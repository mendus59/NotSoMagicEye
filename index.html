How to see one

Look through it

Hold the picture close to your nose so it blurs, then slowly pull it away while keeping your eyes relaxed and unfocused.

Use a reflection

On a glossy screen, focus on your own reflection. The pattern locks into depth behind the glass.

Or let the app do it

Decode matches each strip of the pattern against the next one. Shorter repeats mean nearer surfaces, and that becomes the grey image.
